How we engage

Commercial terms

Engagement shape

  • Pilot first: any Tier "Good" can be preceded by a paid pilot, fee credited against the project if it converts.
  • Billing: 30% on SOW signature, 40% at midpoint, 30% at acceptance. Retainers monthly in advance. Managed local-AI monthly in arrears.
  • Expenses: client hardware and cloud billed at cost + 10%. Hardware normally client-purchased against our spec; alternatively we procure with 10–15% markup.

IP & trust

  • IP: custom code delivered to you; the reusable accelerator stays ours and is licensed for your internal use.
  • Confidentiality & DPA: standard in the MSA; data processed on your hardware remains your property.
  • Warranty: 30 days post-acceptance defect fix included; beyond that on retainer/managed.
