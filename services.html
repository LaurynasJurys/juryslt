Positioning AI deployment properly

Local for privacy and steady workloads. Azure for scale, resilience, and managed operations.

The strongest client conversation is usually not about proving cloud is always better. It is about matching architecture to reality. Local AI can be the better fit for highly sensitive internal use. Azure Kubernetes with GPU becomes more relevant when workloads need scaling, stronger uptime, identity and policy integration, and a clear path from prototype to production service.

Local is often stronger for privacy-first internal workflows and predictable always-on usage. Azure becomes more compelling when AI needs to serve multiple users, integrate with business platforms, scale on demand, and sit inside a governed production environment.
