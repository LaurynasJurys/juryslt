Azure landing zones series

A plain-English blog plan for explaining Azure landing zones.

This series is designed for a general audience, not cloud professionals. The goal is to explain what Azure landing zones are, why businesses should care, and what benefits they bring without drowning people in technical language.

Series goal

Make Azure landing zones understandable to normal business people.

The content should use simple speech, business relevance, and practical examples. Instead of writing for engineers, the writing should help founders, managers, and non-specialists understand why Azure foundations matter.
