About

Practical AI delivery first. Stronger cloud foundations where they help.

Jurys Cloud Consulting exists to help SMBs move from vague AI ambition to usable systems, with Azure architecture and resilience work added where they make the deployment safer, more scalable, or easier to support.

Why this approach exists

Many businesses want to adopt AI quickly, but do not need a generic cloud consultancy story. They need help choosing the right deployment model, fitting it to their privacy and budget constraints, and making sure the result is something the team can actually use.

Sometimes that means local AI. Sometimes it means Azure-based services. Sometimes it means hybrid architecture. The important part is to choose the right model for the business, then add the foundation and resilience work that makes it hold up.

01

Start from the use case

Stay proportional

03

Add cloud and resilience where they strengthen the result

Azure foundations and disaster recovery planning matter when they help AI deployments become more secure, scalable, and supportable.

Positioning

AI deployment and training at the center. Azure and DR in support.

The offer is intentionally centered on AI delivery for SMBs. Cloud platform work and resilience planning are supporting services, used where they help the AI system succeed in production instead of becoming fragile or hard to trust.
