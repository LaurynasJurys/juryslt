Why now

The market is moving to private AI — now.

  • EU AI Act (phased 2025–2027) — high-risk systems need documentation, logging, and human oversight. On-prem + observability is the easiest compliance path.
  • GDPR enforcement is sharper — cross-border data egress is now a documented audit risk. "Run it on our hardware" closes the finding.
  • Open-weight quality jumped — Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral, GLM make self-hosting genuinely competitive with frontier APIs.
  • "Pilot fatigue" — firms ran ChatGPT experiments that never reached production. Budget is shifting from try AI to make AI work, safely.

Who feels the pain

  • Banks & fintech — data can't leave the country.
  • Law & accounting — confidentiality is non-negotiable.
  • Public sector — sovereignty mandate.
  • Manufacturing — process automation, document AI.
  • Healthcare — patient data, MDR/GxP.
